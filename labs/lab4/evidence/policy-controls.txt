Lab4 single-hart policy control experiments
Each control compiles a temporary proc.c and links its temporary proc.o
in place of kernel/proc.o. Formal sources, objects and image stay untouched.
Only policy selection changes; swtch, locks, timer, sleep and wait are shared.
